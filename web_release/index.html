Description

Prayer Times is a native, ultra-lightweight macOS menu bar application designed specifically for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) MacBooks. It lives quietly at the top of your screen next to your Wi-Fi and Battery indicators, providing instant Islamic prayer waqt timings for Dhaka, Bangladesh, live waqt countdowns, prayer check-offs, and an automated Kaza (missed prayer) tracker.

  • ✓ Calibrated for Dhaka: Accurate astronomical prayer calculations based on Islamic Foundation Bangladesh / Karachi standards (18° Fajr & Isha). Supports both Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr methods.
  • ✓ Live Menu Bar Countdown: Glancing at your menu bar shows the active waqt and exact remaining time (e.g. "Asr: 1h 6m left") with a crisp, native vector mosque icon.
  • ✓ Interactive Daily Checklist: Easily mark off Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha with satisfying checkmarks right from the dropdown popover.
  • ✓ Automated Kaza (Missed Prayer) Tracker: If a waqt ends without being marked as prayed, the app flags it as missed, sends a gentle reminder, and logs it in your Kaza backlog counter.
  • ✓ 100% Native Swift & Apple Silicon Optimized: Consumes less than 25MB RAM and near-zero CPU. Runs natively on M1, M2, M3, and M4 Macs with practically zero battery impact.
  • ✓ 100% Offline & Private: Computes all solar equations on-device. Zero internet required, zero tracking, zero background telemetry.
