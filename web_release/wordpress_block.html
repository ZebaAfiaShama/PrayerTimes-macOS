Description

Prayer Times is a native, ultra-lightweight macOS menu bar application designed specifically for Apple Silicon (M1/M2/M3/M4) MacBooks. It lives quietly at the top of your screen next to your Wi-Fi and Battery indicators, providing instant Islamic prayer waqt timings for Dhaka, Bangladesh, live waqt countdowns, prayer check-offs, and an automated Kaza (missed prayer) tracker.

✓ Calibrated for Dhaka: Accurate astronomical prayer calculations based on Islamic Foundation Bangladesh / Karachi standards (18° Fajr & Isha). Supports Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr methods.
✓ Live Menu Bar Countdown: Glancing at your menu bar shows the active waqt and exact remaining time (e.g. "Asr: 1h 6m left") with a crisp native vector mosque glyph.
✓ Interactive Daily Checklist: Easily mark off Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha with satisfying checkmarks right from the dropdown popover.
✓ Automated Kaza (Missed Prayer) Tracker: When a waqt ends without being completed, the app flags it as missed, sends a gentle reminder, and increments your Kaza tally.
✓ 100% Native Swift & Apple Silicon Optimized: Consumes less than 25MB RAM and near-zero CPU. Runs natively with practically zero battery impact.
✓ 100% Offline & Private: Computes all solar equations on-device. Zero internet required, zero tracking, zero background telemetry.
